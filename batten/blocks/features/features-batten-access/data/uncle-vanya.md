Vanya has run his brother-in-law's estate for twenty-five years and sent every kopek of it to the city. This summer the professor comes home to stay, with a young wife nobody can stop looking at, and the house starts to come apart at the seams.

Petra Lund's new version keeps every word of Chekhov's argument and none of the dust. Inés Halvorsen directs the full company, in a birch wood painted on our own paint frame, with the house lights never quite out.
