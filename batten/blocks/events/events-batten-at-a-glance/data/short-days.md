Deb has poured at the Corner for thirty-one years, and tonight she signs it over to Ayaan, who drove a cab past it every night for ten. The regulars have opinions. So does the furnace.

Hodan Ismail wrote Short Days for this company and this neighborhood, after a winter of sitting in its bars with a notebook. It plays in rep with Hedda Gabler, on the nights she doesn't.
