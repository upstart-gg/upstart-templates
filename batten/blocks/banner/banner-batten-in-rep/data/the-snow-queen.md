Kai and Gerda grow roses on the roof between their two attics, until a splinter of the Snow Queen's mirror lodges in Kai's eye and he follows her sledge north. Gerda goes after him, past a crow, a robber girl and a reindeer who knows the way.

The company's own version of Andersen is for anyone six and up. Every cloth in it flies, and the snow comes down on the first three rows.
