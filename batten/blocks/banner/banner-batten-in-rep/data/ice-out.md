Their father left them a fish house, a truck that won't start and a list of who owes him money. Carol wants to sell it, Lisa wants to sink it, and Bee has been living in it since October without telling anyone.

Wren Haugland's first full-length play is a comedy until it isn't. It plays upstairs in the Loft, ninety-six seats around a hole in the ice.
