Hedda has come home from her honeymoon to a house she didn't choose and a husband who adores his research. When the man she nearly married walks in with a manuscript that could make him famous, she decides to have, for once in her life, power over someone's fate.

Arne Vik's translation is fast and dry. Hedda plays in rep with Short Days, on alternate nights: after each of her performances the crew flies her drawing room out and the corner bar in.
