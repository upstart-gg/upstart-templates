Room 12 has a wood floor, a basket of shakers and a rule: nobody sits still for more than four minutes. Children come in with a grown-up the first two weeks, then on their own when they're ready.

They learn to keep a beat with their feet before their hands, to hear high and low, loud and soft, and to wait for their turn, which is the hard part. By spring most can sing a tune back and clap what they hear.

At five, the ones who can't leave the violins alone move to First strings, or to a first half hour alone with a teacher.
