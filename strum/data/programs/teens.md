Teens come for the people as much as the music, so there's a band, a mariachi and a songwriting room to belong to, with or without lessons of their own.

We put bands together by what they want to play, not by level alone, and give them the Carport, a teacher and a date. Band night is in December whether they feel ready or not, and it has never once gone badly enough to stop them.

For All-State, for college or for a first paid wedding, their teacher picks the pieces with them and runs a mock audition in front of two others.
