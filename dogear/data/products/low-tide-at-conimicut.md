Mara Ellison grew up in Warwick, and you can tell: the cottage at Conimicut Point smells of low tide and wet screens, and the three Callahan sisters who come back to empty it after their mother dies talk the way Rhode Islanders talk when they are trying not to cry.

It's a summer book that isn't light. Nobody gets the ending they wanted, and the middle sister, Bridget, made me furious for a hundred pages and then broke my heart. I read the last chapter on the 11 bus and rode it to the end of the line.

We have signed copies, and Mara is launching it here on March 11. Come and ask her about the cottage. — Nadia
