Lionel Okafor is a surveyor who became an essayist, and every chapter starts with a real map: a 1790 chart of Narragansett Bay, a bus map, the plan of a cemetery, a hand-drawn map his grandmother made of a village in Nigeria that no longer has that name.

I carried mail in the West End for thirty years, so the chapter on route maps made me laugh out loud. He's coming to the Grange on March 25. Bring your own favorite map. — Walt
