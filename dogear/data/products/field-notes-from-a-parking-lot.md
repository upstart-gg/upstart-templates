June Takeda spent a year watching the parking lot of the supermarket where she shops: the gulls on the light poles, the chicory in the cracks, and a fox that lives behind the bottle return.

It's funny and very kind, and it made me look at the lot on Cranston Street differently. Short chapters, one a week, good for the bus. We sold the last copy on Saturday: ask, and we'll have it in three days. — Walt
