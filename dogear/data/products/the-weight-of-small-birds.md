Teodor Halas writes very short chapters, and for the first fifty pages almost nothing happens: Jerzy tunes pianos, feeds the sparrows on his sill, and listens to the woman upstairs practice the same Chopin étude every evening.

Then she stops playing, and the book turns into something I didn't see coming. Ewa Brandt's translation is so plain and so exact that you forget it was ever in Polish. This is the one I've been pressing on everyone since October, and it's our translation club's book for March. — Nadia
