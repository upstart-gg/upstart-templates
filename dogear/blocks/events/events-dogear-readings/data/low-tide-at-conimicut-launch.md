Mara Ellison's second novel comes out on Tuesday, and she is launching it at the soda counter, where she wrote a good part of it.

She'll read for twenty minutes, talk with Nadia about the Callahan sisters and the cottage at Conimicut Point, then sign. There will be coffee, lemonade and Portuguese sweet bread.

**About the author.** Mara Ellison grew up in Warwick and teaches writing at a community college. Her first novel, *The Breakwater*, was a staff favorite in 2022.
