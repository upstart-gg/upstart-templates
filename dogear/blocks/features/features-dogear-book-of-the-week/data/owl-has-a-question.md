Owl can't sleep, so Owl goes around the wood asking every animal one question. I won't tell you what it is: the whole fun of reading it aloud is that the kids start guessing by the third page.

The pictures are printed like old linocuts, in two colors, and the last spread is the best bedtime page I've seen this year. For ages 3 to 7, and for any grown-up who reads in voices. Pilar Osei is coming to our Saturday story time on March 13. — Maritza
