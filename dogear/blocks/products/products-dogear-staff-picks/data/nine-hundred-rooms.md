Yes, it's 864 pages. Ansel Whitcombe follows a hotel in a seaside town from the night it opens in 1924 to the morning it closes, a hundred years later, and nearly every chapter takes place in a different room.

You meet a bellhop at sixteen and again at eighty. You learn who was in room 412 the night of the fire. It's the most generous big novel I've read since I stopped teaching, and it's The Long Read's book through May: come for a chapter or for all of it. — Owen
