Ines Carvalho cooked a pot of soup every Tuesday for her family for twenty years, and this is the book of them: fifty-two soups, one a week, with what to do with the leftovers on Wednesday.

Half of them come from the Azores and Cape Verde, and the rest from whatever was cheap at the market. I've made eleven so far. The caldo verde is the one I bring to the shop when it snows. — Maritza
