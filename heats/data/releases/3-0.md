This is the model we set out to make in October 2024: one whose data you can read. Every source is in the manifest, with its license. Where we may share the text, there is a link to download it; where we may not, there is a line that says so.

It took 1.9 million GPU-hours, most of them given by the Saltmarsh Compute Cooperative and Northgate University. At 6 trillion tokens the loss rose for nine days. The cause was a mirror that served the same 11 million pages twice; the report will have the whole story.

## Coming from Dunlin 2

The chat format changed. If you build prompts by hand, use the template that comes with the weights: the old one still answers, less well.
