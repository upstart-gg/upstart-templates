A small release for the people who read contracts with it. The longer context comes from training on 180 billion tokens of long public-domain texts, and it costs nothing in memory until you use it.

The last bracket went missing because our own training data had been cut at a fixed length. We fixed the data, and published the script that looks for this in yours.
