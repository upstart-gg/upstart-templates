Once a year we take the studio three hours north, to a lodge by the river with the Sawtooths in every window. September is the month for it: frost on the grass, aspens turning, nobody on the road.

The classes suit anyone who has been coming for a month or two. Nothing is compulsory, including breakfast.

Lodge meals are vegetarian, with trout on Saturday for those who eat it. Tell us what you don't eat and the kitchen will manage.
