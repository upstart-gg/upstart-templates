Two hours on the poses most classes hurry past. We'll start with wrists, then learn where the weight goes, then spend a long time with a block under the forehead, which is most of the secret.

You should be able to hold a plank for five breaths. You don't need to have left the floor before.

Bring a layer for the rest at the end. Places are held in the app, and moved to someone else if you cancel by Thursday.
