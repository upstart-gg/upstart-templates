A reformer is a sliding bed on springs. The springs hold you up in some exercises and pull against you in others, which makes it kind to backs and honest about habits.

There are eight machines, so the teacher sees every one of you. On a membership or a pack, a Reformer class counts as two; a membership for every class includes four of them a month.
