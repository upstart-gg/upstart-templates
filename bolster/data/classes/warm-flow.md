On these evenings we let the city's hot water take the Long Room to 85°F. Warm, never hot: you'll sweat a little, and nobody leaves dizzy.

The heat loosens the long muscles, so the poses are held longer than in Slow Flow, with fewer of them. We open the windows for the last rest.
