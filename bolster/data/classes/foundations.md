Theo teaches a handful of poses slowly, and the same handful again the next week, until they're yours. You'll stand, fold forward, kneel, and learn what each block and strap is for.

Nobody is asked to touch their toes. There are always three versions of a pose on offer, and the teacher shows the easiest one first.

The last ten minutes are on your back, under a blanket, on a floor that is warm from October to April.
