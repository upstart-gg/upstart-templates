The class is built around one sequence, done three times: once to learn it, once to find the breath in it, once to enjoy it. Between the rounds you rest.

Theo's 6:15 is the quiet one: few words, the lamps down, and you're out of the door by twenty past seven. Maite's classes talk a little more and stand a little longer.
