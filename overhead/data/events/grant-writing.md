Most applications are decided on their first page. Two members who write grants for a living take the evening to go through yours: what the project is, who it's for, what it costs, said in that order.

Bring a project and a page about it, however rough. There are fourteen seats because the Showroom has fourteen chairs. The $15 is paid at the door and goes to the kitchen.
