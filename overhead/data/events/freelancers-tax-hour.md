Once a season a CPA from the neighborhood eats a sandwich in the Showroom and answers whatever comes up. December's questions are the usual ones: the January payment, what counts as an office at home when you also rent a desk, and whether this is the year to hire somebody.

Bring the question, not the shoebox. It's general advice for fourteen people around one table; for your own numbers you'll want an hour of somebody's time, and you'll leave knowing which kind.
