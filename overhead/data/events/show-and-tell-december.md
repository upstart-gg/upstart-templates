Every Thursday, whoever likes shows the rest of us what's on their desk. On the first one of the month we pick six, tell everybody, and leave the door open.

This month: a trail map with a mistake in it, the first ninety seconds of a film about smelt fishing, a chair that came back, a lease in plain English, a box that folds without glue, and a spreadsheet somebody is oddly proud of.

Andre keeps the timer. When it rings, the next person stands up. There's soup from half past six.
