In January the buyers used to come up here to choose the season's chairs. We keep the month and change the offer: on the first Monday of the year, anyone's first desk is free.

Come up, take any desk in the river room, try the booths and the kitchen, and stay until six if it suits you. Lena walks people around at 10 and at 3, tells the story of the floor, and answers what it costs without being asked twice.

Save a seat so we know how much coffee to make.
