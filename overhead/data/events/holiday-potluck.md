The month's last potluck moves up a week so nobody has to choose. Bring one dish with its name on a piece of tape, and a chair if yours is the good one.

We push the hot desks up to the long table at half past three, so finish your calls by then. Families, partners and the calm dogs are all welcome. The dishwasher rota is suspended, which is to say Sam does it.
