Lupe Cárdenas runs Taquería Dos Puentes on 24th Street with her brother and sixteen others. The kitchen works in Spanish, the front in both.

The schedule was a spreadsheet, printed on Fridays and read out loud. “Half the crew couldn’t read it, so I read it to them, and then I corrected it in pen all week,” she says.

## One week, two languages

With Staff Meal, each person chooses their language in the app. The week is the same; the words are theirs. Swaps are offered and taken without Lupe in the middle, and she approves them from her phone between orders.

## Overtime, before it happens

The warning before anyone passes forty hours was what sold her. “I used to find out on the paycheck. Now I find out on Wednesday, when I can still fix it.”
