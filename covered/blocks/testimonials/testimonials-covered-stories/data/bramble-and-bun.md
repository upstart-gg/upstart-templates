Tess Okonjo opened Bramble & Bun on Wornall Road in 2019 with two ovens and four people. By 2023 there were eleven: three bakers who start at 5 am, a counter that runs until 6 pm, and a coffee bar on weekends.

The schedule was a sheet of paper on the walk-in door. “I’d write it on Sunday night, after close, with whatever I remembered from the week’s texts,” Tess says. “Then three people would take a photo of it, and by Wednesday the photo was wrong.”

## The Sunday night

Most of the work wasn’t the schedule. It was the changes. Who could cover Saturday morning, who had a class on Sundays now, who had already said yes to closing Friday in a text nobody else saw. “I had sticky notes on the sticky notes.”

## The first week

Staff Meal’s team typed the sheet in from a photo before the first call. Tess moved two shifts, pressed publish, and the crew had the week on their phones by Thursday. The bakers, who had never installed an app for work, were the first to offer a swap.

## What changed

The swaps now happen in the app, between people who can work the shift, and Tess says yes or no from her phone. The hours from the time clock match the schedule, and the payroll file goes out every other Monday in the format her payroll service asks for.

> “I still write things on sticky notes. They’re just not the schedule anymore.”
