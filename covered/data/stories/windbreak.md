Kelsey Brandt keeps three Windbreak cafés running in Wichita. Twenty-seven baristas, most of them students, many of them in more than one café a week.

Payroll took her a whole Monday every two weeks: paper timecards, a calculator, a spreadsheet for the tips. “I knew every mistake would come back to me on payday.”

## Hours that add up

The baristas clock in on their phones at the door, and declare their tips when they clock out. By the end of the pay period the hours, the overtime and the tips are already there, approved day by day.

## One file

Kelsey checks the totals and exports one file in the format her payroll service reads. “It’s not exciting. That’s the point.”
