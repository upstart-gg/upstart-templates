---
title: "What happens after the trial"
topic: "Billing"
summary: "After 30 days your account waits for a plan. Nothing is charged, and nothing is lost."
updated: "2026-08-18"
---
The trial is 30 days of Full house, with every feature, and we never ask for a card.

At the end of it:

1. Your account waits. Your weeks, hours and crew stay where they are.
2. The crew app keeps showing the last published week.
3. Choose a plan in **Settings** to keep building weeks. You’re billed from that day.

If you don’t choose one, we delete the account 90 days later, after a text to remind you.
