---
title: "Exporting for payroll"
topic: "Timesheets & pay"
summary: "At the end of the pay period, send everyone's approved hours, overtime and tips as one file."
updated: "2026-11-09"
---
Staff Meal adds up the pay period. Your payroll service pays it.

1. Open **Payroll export** at the end of the period.
2. Check the totals: regular hours, overtime and tips per person.
3. Choose the format your payroll service reads, or a plain spreadsheet.
4. Press **Export**, and upload the file where your payroll service asks for it.

If a day isn’t approved yet, the export tells you which one. Past exports stay in the list, a click away.
