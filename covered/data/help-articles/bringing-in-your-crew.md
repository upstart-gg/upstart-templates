title: "Bringing in your crew"
topic: "Getting started"
summary: "Add your people from a list, a spreadsheet or a photo of your schedule, and we send each of them the app."
updated: "2026-10-14"
---
Your crew list is names, roles and mobile numbers. That’s all we need.

1. Go to **Crew** and choose **Add people**.
2. Type them in, paste them from a spreadsheet, or send us a photo of your paper schedule and we’ll type it in for you.
3. Give each person their roles: kitchen, counter, coffee bar, or your own.
4. Press **Send invites**. Each person gets a text with the link to the app.

People who haven’t opened the app yet are marked **Invited**. You can send the text again from their name.
