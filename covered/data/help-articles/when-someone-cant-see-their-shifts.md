Almost always, it’s one of three things.

1. **The week isn’t published.** Drafts are only yours. Check the week’s top bar says **Published**.
2. **Their number changed.** Look at their name in **Crew**. If the number is old, change it and send the invite again.
3. **Notifications are off.** The week is in the app even then. Ask them to open it and pull down to refresh.

Still nothing? Call us at (816) 555-0134 with their name, and we’ll look at it with you.
