---
title: "Approving the day's hours"
topic: "Timesheets & pay"
summary: "Look at the flags, fix what needs fixing, and approve the whole day in one go."
updated: "2026-11-09"
---
Every clock-in sits beside the shift it belongs to. Whatever doesn’t match is flagged.

1. Open **Timesheets** and pick the day.
2. Look at the flags first: early starts, late finishes, missed breaks.
3. Tap a time to fix it, with a note if you want one. The crew member sees the change.
4. Press **Approve day**.

Only approved hours go into the payroll export. A day you haven’t approved stays at the top of the list until you do.
