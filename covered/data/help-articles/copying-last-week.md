title: "Copying last week"
topic: "The schedule"
summary: "Start next week from this one, with its notes, then move what changed."
updated: "2026-11-02"
---
Most weeks look like the last one. So start there.

1. Open next week. It’s empty.
2. Choose **Copy last week**. Every shift comes across, with its notes and its open shifts.
3. Anyone with time off or who can’t work that day is marked in the grid. Move their shifts first.
4. Check the hours column. Anyone close to 40 hours is flagged in coral.
5. Press **Publish** when it looks right.

Nothing reaches the crew until you publish. Changes after publishing are sent as a short update, not a whole new week.
