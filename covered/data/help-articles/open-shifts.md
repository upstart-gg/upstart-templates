---
title: "Open shifts"
topic: "The schedule"
summary: "Post a shift with nobody on it, and let the people who can work it offer to take it."
updated: "2026-09-30"
---
An open shift is a shift with nobody on it yet: an extra Saturday morning, a busy holiday, someone out sick.

1. In the week, choose **Add open shift** on its day.
2. Give it its hours and its role.
3. Publish. Everyone who can work that role, and isn’t already on, sees it in the app.

Offers come back to you as **2 offers**, **3 offers**. Choose one, and the shift is theirs. The others are told it’s taken.
