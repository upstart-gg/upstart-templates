Staff Meal has two doors: one for managers and one for the crew.

## Managers

1. Open the log-in page from the link in your welcome text, and keep it as a bookmark.
2. Type the mobile number you gave us. We text you a six-digit code.
3. Type the code. You stay logged in on that device for 30 days.

On your phone, the same number logs you into the manager side of the app.

## The crew

Your crew never need a password. They tap the link in the text we send them, install the app, and their number logs them in.

If a number changed, call us at (816) 555-0134 and we’ll move the account to the new one.
