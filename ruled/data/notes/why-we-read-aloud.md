Every session starts the same way. It is seven o’clock, people are still finding the mute button, and someone reads a page aloud. For a minute afterward, nobody says anything clever.

I learned to do this at a newspaper, where I was not supposed to have time. The night editor read every lead out loud, under his breath, and if he stopped to breathe in a place you hadn’t planned, he handed it back. He never said what was wrong. He’d found where the sentence lied, and fixing it was my job.

> The ear finds the false sentence before the eye does.

Your eye is kind to you. It knows what you meant, and it fills in the rest. Your ear has no idea what you meant. It hears the word you used twice, the joke you don’t believe, and the place where you started sounding like someone who writes essays.

## How to do it at home

Print the page. Stand up. Read at the speed you would use for a stranger who is interested. Every time you stumble, make one mark and go on. Don’t fix anything.

When you’ve finished, look at the marks. There will be a paragraph with none. That’s the one that’s yours. The rest of the work is getting the other paragraphs to sound as if they had met it.
